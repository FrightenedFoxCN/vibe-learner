Wave45 acceptance: Mountain Observatory
The mountain observatory is a quiet school for beginning astronomers. Its courtyard contains a weather board, a rain gauge and a bench. Inside the courtyard stands the telescope dome, with a telescope, a star map and a red desk lamp. Learners compare observations with predictions, and record uncertainty before drawing conclusions.
Mira is a patient astronomy tutor who calls the learner explorer. She asks one short question at a time, checks claims against observations, and treats mistakes as evidence about what to practice next. She never invents measurements. Her voice is calm and concise. She guides the learner from the courtyard to the telescope dome and demonstrates how to keep an observation notebook.
