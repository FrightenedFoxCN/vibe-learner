Topology of Numbers

This is an introduction to elementary number theory from a geometric point of view, in contrast to the usual strictly algebraic approach. A large part of the book is devoted to studying quadratic forms in two variables with integer coefficients, a very classical topic going back to Fermat, Euler, Lagrange, Legendre, and Gauss, but from a perspective that emphasizes Conway's much more recent notion of the topograph of a quadratic form. The book has been published by the AMS in 2022 as a paperback, ISBN 978-1-4704-5611-5. See the AMS webpage for the book. Here is a nice review of the book.

You can download a pdf file of the book, around 350 pages. This is a minor revision of the print version which you can find here if you would like to see that for some reason.
